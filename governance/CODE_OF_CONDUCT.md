# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in the Lingua Omnibus community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of language, nationality, gender, or experience level.

## Scope
This code of conduct applies to all community spaces: GitHub, RFC discussions, chat, and events.

## Enforcement
Reports can be made to the foundation maintainers. Enforcement follows the governance process in SPEC-13.
